Overview

Built for how you actually work

Custom platforms for dental coaching and practice-management firms — assessments, scorecards, CRM and KPI tracking that scale your methodology beyond the number of hours your coaches have.

Coaching and consulting firms in dentistry hit a hard ceiling. The methodology works and clients get results, but delivery depends on senior people running assessments, building spreadsheets and preparing for calls. Revenue scales with headcount, quality varies with whoever is delivering, and nobody can see across the client base whether an account is drifting until it does not renew.

We turn the programme into a platform: standardized assessments with benchmarking, coaching scorecards fed by real practice-management data, action plans that are re-measurable, and cohort views so coaches manage a book rather than a calendar. One platform we built on this model now runs 800+ practices.

Scaling a methodology

What changes when coaching becomes software

Delivery stops depending on who shows up. Every client receives the full methodology in the right order, whether they are working with your founder or your newest coach. That consistency is what makes the programme a product rather than a collection of relationships.

Reviews start from agreed numbers. When KPIs arrive automatically from the practice-management system, the first ten minutes of every call stops being a reconciliation argument. Coaches spend their time on interpretation and accountability, which is what clients are actually paying for.

Churn becomes visible early. Practices that stop engaging, miss milestones or show slipping numbers surface as flags a quarter before renewal. Coaching churn is rarely sudden — it is just usually unobserved.

Your results become provable. Aggregate outcomes across the client base — average production growth, retention, benchmark movement — turn into the firm's strongest sales asset. Being able to show a prospect the actual dashboard their practice would get closes deals that a testimonial does not.

Capacity per coach rises. The firm we built for did not add coaches proportionally to reach 800+ practices. The routine delivery moved into the product and the coaches went back to coaching.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you turn our coaching methodology into software?

Yes — it's one of our favorite builds. We take the assessments, scorecards, and workflows your firm delivers by hand and build them into a role-based platform, so your methodology is applied consistently and reaches far more practices. We built a platform 800+ practices now run on.

Do the practices we coach get their own logins?

They can. We build client-facing portals so practices log in, complete assessments, track progress, and see their KPIs — turning periodic check-ins into an always-on relationship.

Can it pull real numbers from the practices' systems?

Yes — we integrate with practice-management systems and build the pipelines that bring production, collection, and A/R data into your platform, so coaching is based on real performance.

We're a small firm — is a custom platform overkill?

Not if scaling is the goal. The point of building it once is to stop doing it by hand for every client. We scope to where you are, build something that grows with you, and run it.

We are consultants, not a software company. Can we really own a platform?

Yes, and most of our clients here started from exactly that position. You own the methodology and the client relationships; we handle product, engineering and ongoing operation as a build-and-run partner. The firms that struggle are the ones who commission a build and then have nobody to maintain it, which is the arrangement we deliberately avoid.

Will the platform work with our clients' different practice-management systems?

Yes. Your client practices will run a mix — Open Dental, Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Denticon, CareStack — and the integration layer normalizes them so scorecards populate automatically regardless. That matters commercially too: onboarding a new client becomes a connection rather than a data-collection project.
