Security & compliance

Healthcare-grade security, built into everything we ship.

Most dev shops treat compliance as paperwork at the end. The data dental companies hand us is protected health information — your buyers will ask how it's handled. So we build to dental-grade standards from the first commit, and we can prove it.

SOC 2 Type II

Audited over a monitoring period — not a one-day snapshot — across security, availability, and confidentiality. Report under NDA.

HIPAA compliant

Engineered for PHI end to end, with signed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) on every engagement that touches patient data.

Encryption everywhere

TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest — across every service, database, and backup.

Least-privilege access

Role-based access with SSO and MFA, scoped credentials, no standing access to PHI.

Audit logging

Every access and change to patient data is logged, retained, and traceable.

Secure SDLC

Mandatory code review, dependency and secret scanning, controlled deploys.

North America data residency

PHI stays in US/Canada cloud regions. No offshore data handling, ever.

Vendor & subprocessor control

Every subprocessor vetted, the chain of BAAs kept intact — nothing leaks out the side door.

SOC 2 Type II report and our security overview are available to qualified buyers under NDA — ask on your free discovery call.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who does Webstrail work with?

Dental-industry companies in North America — DSOs and multi-location groups, membership and benefits platforms, practice-management and coaching firms, dental-tech founders, GPOs, labs, and teledentistry.

Do you only build for dental?

It's what we're known for and where we go deep. We build, integrate, modernize, and run all kinds of dental software — and take the occasional project beyond dental for companies we already work with.

Do you build new products, or work on existing software?

Both. From-scratch builds, rebuilds and modernization, new features and AI, PMS integrations, and dedicated engineering when you need extra hands.

Do you run what you build?

Yes. Most engagements continue as a build-and-run retainer — we operate, improve, and extend the product long after launch. It's the part most teams skip.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Absolutely. We can take full ownership end to end, or embed with your existing product and engineering team — whatever fits your roadmap and workflow.

Can you integrate with our practice-management system?

Yes — including Open Dental, Dentrix, and Eaglesoft, plus others through their APIs and clearinghouse connections.

How do engagements start, and what do they cost?

Most begin with a free discovery call, then a short discovery sprint that scopes the work — and the cost — before any build commitment. From there it usually becomes a build-and-run engagement, priced to the work rather than a fixed package.
