The problem

Tooth Teller had a clear idea for a dental product and the domain insight to back it — but no engineering team to build it. Off-the-shelf tools couldn't express their model, and hiring and managing developers would have pulled the founders away from the business they actually knew.

They needed a team that already understood dental: the workflows, the compliance, and the practice-management systems any serious dental product has to integrate with from day one.

What we built

We designed and built the core platform end to end, with Open Dental integration sitting at the heart of it. Then we did the part most shops skip — we stayed and ran it, releasing improvements on a roadmap month after month.

"They understand our business better than any vendor we've worked with — and two years in, they still run it with us."

That two-year retainer is the heart of the story. It's not a project that ended at launch; it's a product we operated as if it were our own.
