Circular avatar for testimonials and people. Photo when available, solid-color initials otherwise.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Dr. Lena Ruiz" src="/people/lena.jpg" size="lg" />
<Avatar name="Tooth Teller" tone="azure" />
```

Sizes `sm | md | lg | xl`. Tones (initials fallback): `warm | sky | azure | amber`. `ring` adds a contrast halo for stacks.
