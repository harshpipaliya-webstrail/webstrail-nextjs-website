Candidate Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the work environment like at Webstrail?

Webstrail is a remote-first engineering team. We prioritize clarity, async communication, outcome-driven delivery, and high technical quality over micromanagement or unnecessary meetings.

Do I need prior dental software experience to apply?

Prior dental or healthcare experience is helpful, but not required for engineering positions. We value strong software fundamentals, problem-solving skills, and a desire to build high-reliability systems.

What is Webstrail's interview process?

Our interview process is streamlined: 1) Initial 30-minute intro call to discuss background and role fit, 2) 60-minute technical interview covering system architecture and past work, 3) Final conversation with leadership.

What benefits and perks does Webstrail offer?

We offer competitive salaries, annual performance bonuses, flexible remote work policies, home-office equipment stipends, health insurance, learning allowances, and generous PTO.
