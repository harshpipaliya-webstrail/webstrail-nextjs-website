Overview

What dental education software means for your business

Training in a dental group is usually invisible until it is a problem. A new hire is shown the practice's way of doing things by whoever happens to be free that morning. Continuing education certificates live in a folder, or in someone's email. Annual compliance training is chased by spreadsheet a fortnight before an audit. It works, roughly, until the group reaches a size where roughly stops being acceptable.

Dental education and LMS software makes the whole thing visible and consistent. Courses, onboarding paths, CE tracking, compliance modules, quizzes and certificates — assigned by role and location, tracked automatically, and reportable when a regulator or an insurer asks.

We build these for DSOs standardizing training across locations, and for dental education companies whose product is the learning platform and who need it to handle CE accreditation, cohorts and commerce properly.

What it covers

From new-hire onboarding to accredited CE

Onboarding that is the same everywhere. Role-specific paths for a new dental assistant, front-desk hire or associate — the group's clinical protocols, systems training, and compliance requirements in a defined sequence with a completion date. This is the fastest return for a growing group, because it makes acquired practices consistent with the rest of the estate quickly.

Compliance, tracked automatically. Infection control, OSHA, HIPAA and state requirements assigned by role, with renewal cycles, automatic reminders and a live view of who is current. The moment this exists, preparing for an audit stops being a project.

Continuing education with certificates. Course delivery, assessment, credit tracking and certificate generation. For accredited providers we build to the requirements of the accrediting body — completion criteria, minimum durations, record retention — because getting that wrong invalidates the credit.

Content that suits clinical learning. Video, interactive modules, downloadable protocols, and assessments that actually test comprehension. Clinical procedure content is largely visual, so playback quality, mobile behaviour and resumability matter more here than in most corporate LMS deployments.

Reporting that answers real questions. Completion by location, role and individual; overdue compliance by practice; and engagement data that shows which content is working. Leadership generally wants one answer — is every location compliant right now — and the platform should give it without anyone assembling a spreadsheet.

Commerce, for education businesses. Where the platform is the product: catalogues, subscriptions, group licensing, and B2B sales to practices and DSOs.

Why custom

Why build dental education software instead of buying a tool

Off-the-shelf tools assume every dental business is the same. They're not — your PMS mix, payers, and workflows are specific, and a generic tool forces you to change how you work to fit it. A custom build does the opposite: it fits you, integrates with what you already run, and belongs to you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why not use an off-the-shelf LMS?

If your need is generic corporate training, an off-the-shelf LMS is genuinely the cheaper answer and we will tell you so. Custom becomes worthwhile when dental specifics matter — CE accreditation rules, clinical protocol content, role-based assignment across many locations, integration with HR and practice systems, or when the platform is a product you intend to sell.

Can it handle CE credits and accreditation requirements?

Yes. We build to the accrediting body's requirements — completion criteria, minimum engagement time, assessment thresholds, certificate generation and record retention. Those rules are specific and getting them wrong invalidates the credit, so they are treated as hard requirements rather than configuration.

How does it help with compliance across multiple locations?

Compliance modules are assigned by role and location with renewal cycles and automatic reminders, and leadership gets a live view of who is current and who is overdue. The practical effect is that audit preparation stops being a fortnight of chasing and becomes a report someone runs.

Can we sell courses to other practices?

Yes — that is a common brief from dental education companies. The platform supports catalogues, individual and group purchasing, subscriptions and B2B licensing to practices and DSOs, alongside the delivery and CE tracking.

Does it integrate with our HR or practice-management systems?

It can. Pulling the staff roster from HR means new hires are assigned their onboarding path automatically and leavers are deactivated without anyone remembering to do it, which is usually where manual LMS administration breaks down in a group of any size.
