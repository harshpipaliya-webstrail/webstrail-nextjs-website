Overview

The bespoke work other shops won't take

Some of the most valuable dental software does not fit a category — production AI, teledentistry, education platforms, GPO portals, a founder's first MVP — and those are the builds most agencies either decline or learn on your budget.

The common thread in this service line is that there is no product to buy. A group purchasing organization needs a member portal with contracted pricing and provable savings. A dental-tech founder needs a real MVP with PMS integration and PHI handling, not a prototype. An education company needs CE accreditation rules encoded correctly. A software provider needs an extra engineering team that already understands dentistry rather than one that needs six months of context.

We take those on end to end: AI imaging and treatment-plan assistance in production rather than in a demo, HIPAA-compliant teledentistry, education and LMS platforms, GPO member portals, and fast, SEO-ready websites that book patients into the PMS. Designed, built, and then run — because specialized software with nobody maintaining it has a short life.

How these engagements work

What it takes to build dental software nobody has built before

Start by scoping honestly. Most specialized builds arrive as a broad ambition. A short discovery sprint turns it into a defined first release with a cost attached, and occasionally concludes that the thing should not be built at all, or should be bought. We would rather deliver that conclusion in week three than in month nine.

Ship something real early. A working slice in production beats a comprehensive specification. For founders this matters commercially — a usable MVP raises money and attracts customers in a way that a deck does not.

Assume compliance from line one. Anything touching patient data needs HIPAA-grade handling, audit logging and access control built in structurally. Retrofitting compliance is dramatically more expensive than designing for it, and for AI and teledentistry it also constrains what the product may legally do — which is worth establishing before, not after, the build.

Plan for who runs it. The question we ask early is who maintains this in eighteen months. If the answer is nobody, that shapes the architecture. Most clients keep us on as a build-and-run partner; some use us to reach a point where hiring an in-house team makes sense, which is a perfectly good outcome and one we will help plan.
